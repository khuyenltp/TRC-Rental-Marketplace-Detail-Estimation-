Project Introduction

Product context and phase boundaries

Product

TRC Rental Marketplace (Maldives) connects renters with TRC inventory and third-party sellers. The platform supports discovery, availability, bookings, payments, handover and rental support across atolls and islands.

The scope covers a responsive renter website, seller portal, marketplace administration and TRC store operations.

Delivery Scope

Phase 1: launch the MVP with the core rental workflow and 366 hours of project-wide work.

Phase 2: add advanced discovery, messaging, seller tools, automation and demand analytics. The 550-hour estimate covers the listed features only.

English interface. MVR is the primary transaction currency, with USD displayed for visitors.

Assumptions And Out Of Scope

  • Responsive web for renters and sellers, with web administration. English only. Native iOS and Android apps are excluded.
  • Payment Option A applies: the marketplace collects online payments and pays sellers. One local payment gateway (BML or MIB), bank transfer with proof upload, and cash at pickup are included.
  • Seller deposits are optional. TRC listings have no deposit at launch. Premium Protection is included in the TRC rental price. Deposit collection and refund rules, protection coverage, and any excess payable by renters must be confirmed during scoping.
  • No commission is charged at launch. The commission settings module is included in Phase 2.
  • MVP keyword searches and seller-contact clicks are logged. Full event tracking and analytics are included in Phase 2. Data retention requirements must be confirmed during scoping to support historical reporting.
  • Phase 2 covers 550 hours for the explicitly estimated features only. Native mobile apps, other unestimated extensions, and additional Phase 2 design, project management, QA/UAT, and deployment work are excluded pending separate estimation.
